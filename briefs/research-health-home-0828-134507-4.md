# Health and home opportunity research

- food-log-export-kit — diet-log users are blocked from leaving broken trackers when exports fail or lose structure.
- calorie-week-view — people want flexible weekly interpretation rather than another daily streak-and-database tracker.
- set-note-progression — set-specific context and transparent progression rules remain missing from everyday workout logs.
- health-data-bridge — activity and nutrition apps repeatedly request a narrow, inspectable Health Connect import boundary.
- flex-meal-templates — repeat meals must accommodate real portion changes and per-meal targets without multiplying entries.
- recipe-import-repair — recipe imports fail opaquely; a deterministic repair preview is more useful than an AI rewrite.
- recipe-library-move-check — privacy-minded households cannot confidently switch recipe systems without a migration preflight.
- family-meal-lanes — multi-person meal grids need to distinguish individual and shared meals at a glance.
- shopping-list-handoff — cooks need to hand off usable lists to people outside their chosen app or account system.
- storage-aware-expiry — freezer/pantry dates only work when they understand where an item is stored.
- home-asset-lineage — appliances and components need independent locations plus traceable maintenance history.
- bank-import-receipts — local budget adoption stalls when bank imports provide neither errors nor balance proof.
