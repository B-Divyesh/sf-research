# Accessibility research — 2026-08-27

remote-web-task-recipes — browser-based remote and legacy work surfaces still strand users when semantic accessibility APIs are absent.

android-select-speak-canvas — people need a fast local way to hear text rendered as inaccessible pixels in games, streams, and canvases.

caption-lens — shared experiences need a personal, highly legible live-caption display without account setup or recording.

caption-cues — captions exist but still need viewer-controlled hierarchy for words and cues that are hard to hear.

transcript-study-view — text-first learners are still manually cleaning platform transcripts just to study a talk accessibly.

caption-source-check — users cannot reliably tell whether a live stream exposes an official usable caption track.

low-vision-workspace-profiles — knowledge work needs saved readability controls rather than uniform zoom that destroys context.

color-meaning-lens — color-only statuses and code diffs remain a direct barrier for color-blind technical workers.

dyslexia-form-guard — high-stakes web forms need a local, calm final check for transpositions and field mistakes.

reading-rail — readers need an in-place focus guide, not just a buried special-font preference.

interview-recall-deck — ADHD-related preparation and memory barriers can turn an interview into an avoidable access barrier.

next-step-cards — people ask for executive-function support but want to retain judgment rather than outsource it to AI.
