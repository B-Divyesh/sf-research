# Education opportunity research — 2026-08-27

exam-deadline-map — learners explicitly need a time-capped route from today’s reviews to a real exam date.
time-budget-new-cards — fixed card quotas fail when new material varies dramatically in difficulty.
importance-weighted-review — high-frequency and high-stakes knowledge needs a visible priority policy, not one retention target.
review-backlog-forecast — returning learners need to see the workload consequences before rescheduling an overdue queue.
focus-study-sprint — users are actively looking for finite, calm active recall without streak mechanics or AI churn.
audio-gap-loop — parents and learners need controlled repeat-and-speak audio practice without an addictive course layer.
parallel-ebook-reader — a learner directly asked for local synchronized bilingual EPUB, audio, and word capture.
no-ai-language-path — there is a direct 2026 request for structured interactive language learning without generative AI.
walk-talk-rehearsal — walking learners want accessible speaking rehearsal without the cost or privacy trade-offs of a live tutor.
skill-drill-studio — procedural skills stick through immediate drills, but lightweight authoring is still awkward.
classroom-algorithm-cards — one-off elementary computing lessons need printable, collaborative sequence/debug activities.
comprehensible-input-log — self-learners need a source-agnostic way to select input they can mostly understand and track progress.
