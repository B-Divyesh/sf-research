# Games & creative opportunity research — 2026-08-27

songsketch-anykey — browser composers outgrow scale-bound music toys but do not need a full DAW.

rhythm-pedal-tidy — pedal-recorded MIDI creates cleanup friction precisely where practice needs quick feedback.

pad-light-choreographer — physical pad controllers deserve instant, light-led practice rather than DAW setup.

session-layout-capsule — creative control layouts are repeated session setup that current projects do not preserve well.

web-audio-route-map — open web-audio demos need an understandable, shareable view of their signal path.

frequency-feel — learners need to hear and see filter/EQ effects before parameter trial-and-error becomes harmful or confusing.

mechanism-playground — people explicitly want mechanical movements turned into approachable building-block play.

pixel-tile-mask — pixel-game makers repeatedly hand-trace technical masks from artwork.

proof-motion-canvas — visual explanations need inspectable timed steps, not just opaque videos or static diagrams.

coop-boss-access — co-op mechanics and depth-perception-friendly cues can be designed together in a small local game.

persistent-ink-cues — presenters need emphasis marks that persist intentionally and work on slow-refresh ink devices.

stream-access-cues — keyboard-first streamers need an independent local control path when embedded platform UIs are inaccessible.
