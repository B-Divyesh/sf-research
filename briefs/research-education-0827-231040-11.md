# Education research — 2026-08-27

context-recall-cards — Vocabulary learners want personal context and pronunciation practice, not another streak.

recall-calibrator — Self-graded flashcards need a way to expose whether the grade signal is trustworthy.

knowledge-note-bridge — Markdown-to-card workflows still destroy review history when knowledge notes evolve.

study-material-import-check — Calm, learner-owned study materials need a safe on-ramp instead of opaque importer failures.

code-prediction-lab — AI-assisted coding learning needs prediction and reproducibility, not more generated explanations.

accessible-explanation-checkin — Schools need accessible evidence of reasoning rather than unreliable AI-detection rituals.

rubric-feedback-bundles — Teachers need faster human feedback that preserves complete, specific student-facing comments.

claim-source-trail — Undergraduates need to learn how a claim connects to an exact source passage before drafting.

transcript-study-slicer — Text-first learners need clean, private study passages from transcripts they can already access.

skill-decision-drills — Branching decision rehearsal transfers complex skills better than linear fact quizzes.

parameter-playground — Interactive explainers need accessible underlying data and prediction prompts, not just visuals.

math-circle-board — Tiny enrichment groups need a privacy-light record of partial strategies and session continuity.
