agent-diff-gate — Agent-authored PR volume is outpacing small-team review, while developers explicitly request a first-class diff-review surface.

team-agent-skills — Shared agent behavior is becoming an SDLC dependency, and standard skill-location support has drawn 454 reactions.

freelancer-agent-context — Freelancers need client boundaries as AI speed changes delivery expectations and multi-account connector demand grows.

agent-capacity-ledger — Paid coding-agent limits fail unexpectedly while teams now operate a fragmented stack of AI infrastructure and subscriptions.

project-memory-release — Teams want project knowledge inside coding agents but are resisting another opaque, overgrown knowledge workspace.

portable-agent-workspaces — Agent configuration is spreading across machines and tools faster than ordinary dotfile workflows can safely carry it.

lsp-readiness-check — High demand for automatic LSP support exposes a prerequisite gap for safe agent changes in unfamiliar repositories.

agent-change-recovery — Larger, more numerous agent-era PRs make selective recovery of a bad multi-step change a daily need.

spoken-dev-brief — Developers want portable voice capture near their tools, but current meeting notes are trapped and detached from implementation decisions.

byok-code-policy — BYOK demand is growing alongside AI-driven QA risk, creating room for policy evidence rather than vendor lock-in.

agent-protocol-console — MCP/ACP adoption is accelerating while teams still lack a practical view of changing agent capabilities and lifecycle events.

agent-task-packets — Buyers burned by SaaS evaluations need safe representative proof, and portable agent-skill standards make reusable task packets timely.
