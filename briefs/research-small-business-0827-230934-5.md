# Small-business opportunity research — 2026-08-27

invoice-approval-gate — Small teams need a simple pre-send check because incorrect quotes and invoices damage trust immediately.
deposit-drawdown-ledger — Formal deposits are common, but showing their remaining balance against later work is still manual.
split-pass-through-costs — One supplier bill often mixes reimbursable and internal costs, forcing duplicate or lossy bookkeeping.
end-client-reference — Subcontractors need invoices to state both who pays and who the work was actually performed for.
quote-payment-trail — Partial deliveries and payments leave owners manually reconstructing a job’s financial story.
supplier-due-board — Tiny businesses want a clear paid/unpaid supplier-bill view without adopting accounts-payable software.
compliance-evidence-pack — Cross-border freelancers need privacy-respecting accountant handoff packets before stressful deadlines.
relationship-safe-payment-followup — Existing reminders are too blunt, so owners inconsistently chase invoices or let them slide.
service-schedule-drift — When one service job moves, solo operators still have to manually rebuild downstream promises.
inventory-promise-hold — Scarce stock can be promised twice during order entry before a standard inventory system reserves it.
client-request-quote-sheet — Repeat clients need a low-friction way to request custom work without a storefront or portal overhaul.
field-time-invoice-proof — AI-assisted freelance work makes timers less honest while clients still need a clear work-to-invoice explanation.
