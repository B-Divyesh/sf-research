# Small-business opportunity research — 2026-08-27

scope-variation-board — freelancers need an explicit client-approved record when a fixed quote changes.
quote-decision-log — tiny teams need a minimal review-and-decision trail before a quote becomes a commitment.
delivery-acceptance-receipt — independent workers need fair delivery evidence before an invoice dispute becomes a payment chase.
invoice-send-ledger — draft and send dates are still easy to blur in otherwise overbuilt invoicing tools.
invoice-evidence-pack — cross-border compliance work is still assembled from folders, sheets, and accountant messages.
payment-cadence — late-payment follow-up is recurrent emotional labor, and generic automation does not fit every client relationship.
billable-review — manually moving hours from timers to invoices still consumes hours and leaves revenue unbilled.
booking-intake-vault — small service teams need booking intake with least-privilege access to client details.
appointment-capacity-map — conditional overlap rules are an underserved small-team scheduling problem.
local-records-continuity — local/self-hosted business software needs a recoverable, understandable backup handoff.
stock-label-run — receiving stock into correct barcode labels remains expensive and context-losing for small sellers.
small-business-export-map — CSV exports still fail at the accountant/client handoff because locale and field assumptions are hidden.
