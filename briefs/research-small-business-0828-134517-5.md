# Small-business opportunity research — 2026-08-28

- ap-ready-invoice-packet — email-first freelancers need an AP-ready invoice artifact, not a full billing suite.
- peppol-preflight — structured e-invoicing mandates make an offline, explainable pre-submission check timely.
- cleared-funds-chaser — promised payment and cleared cash remain different states for transfer-paid work.
- reschedule-recovery — cancellations and reschedules are the small-business scheduling failure mode overlooked by reminders.
- quote-request-capture — customer requests still require retyping through forms, proposals and invoice tools.
- site-job-proof — tradespeople need to preserve job evidence before it disappears into a phone gallery or generic attachment list.
- worklog-appendix — a readable work summary can prevent invoice-hour disputes without replacing time tracking.
- quote-section-builder — multi-part jobs force businesses to recreate accounting quotes in a separate client-readable document.
- client-handoff-kit — completed projects need a portable, organized delivery instead of a collection of SaaS links.
- early-payment-terms — cash-discount terms remain a manual-credit and audit-trail gap in small-business invoicing.
- pocket-cashflow-calendar — irregular receivables and scheduled bills need a simple private forecast, not a finance super-app.
- offline-stocktake-labels — small inventory owners need privacy-preserving labels and count reconciliation without warehouse ERP.
