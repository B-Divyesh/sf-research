# Health / home research — 2026-08-27

- sedentary-time-ledger — wearable users explicitly cannot get a usable daily inactivity total, while existing fitness logs still struggle with low-friction measurement.
- lift-receipt-log — committed lifters are abandoning feature-heavy trackers for notebooks because entering a single set is too slow.
- care-dose-board — families need a shared, privacy-respecting record that proves whether an elderly relative’s dose was actually given.
- taper-calendar-card — a niche but safety-conscious taper schedule is awkward in ordinary repeat-reminder apps and is now requested in open-source medication tooling.
- recipe-exit-pack — recipe capture is thriving, but cross-platform migration still means manual conversion and lost media.
- cookbook-print-run — people continue to ask recipe systems for printable cookbook output; selected, screen-free cooking packets are a focused wedge.
- pantry-reconcile — duplicate buying and expiry waste persist because perfect inventory maintenance is unrealistic for shared homes.
- garden-bed-seasons — gardeners need both local-season timing and a durable memory of what occupies each bed.
- family-weekboard — privacy-conscious cross-platform families are still overwhelmed by account-bound shared-calendar choices.
- chore-proof-calendar — a household calendar is not enough when nobody can tell whether a recurring job was actually completed.
- home-service-history — repair details remain scattered after the job, making repeat maintenance, warranties, and handoffs needlessly difficult.
- daily-safe-to-spend — manual budgeters reject costly bank-sync subscriptions but still want an understandable cash forecast for today.
