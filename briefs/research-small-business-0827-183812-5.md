# Small-business research — 2026-08-27

quote-acceptance-packet — Small operators need an immutable scope/acceptance record between sending a quote and billing it.

retainer-release-ledger — Deposits and delivery gates create a narrow need for a per-job release decision ledger.

subclient-job-map — Subcontracted work needs a lightweight way to distinguish payer, end client, and delivery contact.

late-payment-casefile — Chasing is common, but automatic late-payment behaviour can be legally unsafe; retain a reviewed case record.

reschedule-proof — Cancellations and reschedules break at state handoff, not merely at sending a reminder.

receipt-tax-packet — Sole traders need original-receipt integrity and an accountant-ready handoff without an expense platform.

cashflow-commitments — Near-term cash decisions still require manually combining payouts, invoices, and recurring bills.

barcode-shelf-card — Spreadsheet-scale stockholders need fast shelf-side exception capture rather than an inventory ERP.

purchase-receiving-pad — Partial/damaged deliveries need an independent receiving record, even where the order lives elsewhere.

checklist-evidence-binder — Repeated checks become audit risk when evidence remains scattered through folders and spreadsheets.

client-offboarding-kit — Client closeouts need a portable, security-conscious ownership and access handoff packet.

offline-payment-matchbox — Offline invoicing creates a local CSV reconciliation gap between issued invoices and actual payments.
