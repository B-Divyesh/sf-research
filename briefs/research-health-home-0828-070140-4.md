# Research health-home — 2026-08-28

- med-handoff-card — caregiver medication coordination needs a printable, exception-aware current-dose handoff without clinical claims.
- symptom-visit-brief — people with intermittent symptoms need a concise factual timeline for appointments, not another wellness dashboard.
- durable-set-log — workout sets must survive offline use and PWA reloads; current optimistic-only logs can silently lose them.
- flex-calorie-ledger — activity-aware, no-account weekly food planning remains underserved by rigid daily-target trackers.
- low-energy-menu — family meal plans break when they ignore cook energy, school meals, repetition, and leftovers.
- recipe-source-card — transparent source-preserving recipe capture removes retyping without becoming a copyrighted recipe database.
- glance-cart — shopping lists are still too unlock-heavy; a lock-screen aisle mode targets the repeated in-store interaction.
- fair-turn — adult households need fair recurring chore rotation with absences and swaps, not gamified child task charts.
- house-history-pack — repairs, permits, warranties, and service history need a local exportable house packet.
- receipt-to-room — local receipt OCR can remove the bulk-entry barrier to useful room-level home inventory.
- month-ahead-cash — a dated, no-bank-link cash runway serves a narrower need than a full budgeting platform.
- bench-bin-bom — makers waste time and money when a project BOM cannot be checked against the parts already in drawers.
