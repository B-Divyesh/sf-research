class-capacity-truth — small schools lose revenue when displayed group capacity is wrong and waitlists are manual.
clinic-slot-safety — independent clinics need safe, explainable rescheduling without replacing their EHR.

ap-ready-invoice — freelancers need invoices that survive corporate AP handoff and make the next action visible.
subcontractor-margin-chain — agencies need one commercial chain from end client through subcontractor cost and collection.
client-intake-proof — service firms need a proof packet that a client engagement is truly ready to begin.
cross-client-demand-desk — agencies need to prioritize incoming work across client projects by commercial impact.
multicurrency-vat-close — existing Stripe SaaS businesses need reconciled tax/FX evidence without a disruptive MoR migration.
field-job-proof-pack — solo tradespeople need dispute-resistant closeout proof without a costly field-service suite.
service-reminder-receipts — recurring-service businesses need evidence that critical visit reminders actually arrived.
decision-receipt-inbox — small teams need feedback to produce an owned decision and customer-visible outcome.
