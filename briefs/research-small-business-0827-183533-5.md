# Small-business opportunity research — 2026-08-27

- scope-deposit-ledger — deposits and retainers still fall between a scope promise and a final invoice.
- billable-receipt-split — contractors need one receipt split cleanly across jobs before month-end bookkeeping.
- early-pay-terms — early-payment discounts are common but remain error-prone in otherwise full-featured invoicing tools.
- quote-to-cash-trail — a client-readable path from quote to payment is missing from overgrown finance suites.
- subcontract-client-brief — subcontractors need to distinguish the paying client from the end customer at handoff.
- payout-bundle-matcher — payment-processor deposits continue to obscure the sales, fees, refunds, and timing beneath them.
- actuals-job-sequencer — tiny field teams need a schedule that moves when real work, rather than the plan, changes.
- booking-side-notes — operational notes should not consume appointment capacity.
- service-notification-router — small multi-service offices need booking notices routed to the responsible person, not every inbox.
- shared-capacity-slots — mixed services need valid slots based on people and resources rather than one overblocked calendar.
- backfill-timecards — freelancers increasingly reconstruct non-linear AI-era work after the fact before invoicing.
- shelf-walk-stocktake — physical counts need a local, shelf-ordered variance pass that works alongside legacy systems.
