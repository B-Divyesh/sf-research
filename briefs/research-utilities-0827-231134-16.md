# Utilities research — 2026-08-27

local-file-triage — Existing-folder cleanup needs a reversible, local review queue rather than opaque auto-sorting.
bookmark-merge-map — Desktop/mobile bookmark divergence creates a risky merge-and-dedupe job with no reliable dry run.
backup-coverage-ledger — Teams fail on visibility and restoreability, not merely on scheduling backup jobs.
message-archive-audit — Account/device loss makes attachment-complete, locally verifiable message archives urgent.
critical-alert-lane — People need a small acknowledge-until-done reminder lane outside notification overload.
scan-archive-receipt — Digitizers need provenance, checksums, and batch order before a years-long curation project.
accessible-photo-catalog — Low-vision users need a large-type, keyboard-first local photo triage workflow.
csv-keyed-diff — Business CSV reconciliation breaks under row reordering; key-based field changes are the actual unit of work.
tabular-file-lens — Fast, private CSV inspection remains underserved by ad-heavy web viewers and terminal formatting tools.
review-pdf-packet — External reviewers need comments and decisions bundled with a PDF without source-workspace access.
phone-photo-intake — Cross-device transfer still fails in practice; a receipt enables a safe deletion decision.
offline-ledger-import — Private finance users need a format-neutral CSV import and balance-reconciliation checkpoint.
