# Venture research — 2026-08-28

- mtd-quarterly-ready — UK MTD deadlines create a near-term, recurring compliance wedge for sole traders who find full accounting tools too heavy.
- accountant-close-pack — Small business owners need a CPA-ready evidence package without remaining trapped in their accounting vendor.
- client-action-room — Agencies repeatedly stitch together portals because clients need a short, accountable queue of approvals, uploads, and payments.
- worklog-approval-bridge — Freelance developers need privacy-preserving reconstruction of billable work and a client approval record.
- clinic-reminder-proof — Clinics need observable reminder delivery and fallback when EMR, calendar, or messaging providers fail.
- field-parts-promise — Trades firms need part-to-job allocation before making customer date promises, not another bloated field-service suite.
- receipt-recovery — Small operators need reliable offline receipt capture and proof-to-transaction matching for every deductible expense.
- purchase-intake-desk — Smaller warehouses need dependable PO receiving and discrepancy evidence without an ERP rollout.
- batch-proofbook — Micro-manufacturers need recall-ready batch proof and retention at a price below industrial QMS/MRP products.
- service-proof-loop — Recurring service businesses can turn job proof, feedback, and extras directly into the next visit.
- integration-handoff-room — Agencies need client-accepted API handoff artifacts that connect docs, fixtures, and ownership.
- spend-commitment-radar — Tiny teams need private ownership and evidence around recurring commitments before surprise renewals.
