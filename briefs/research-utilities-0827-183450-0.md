# Utilities research — 2026-08-27

bookmark-import-audit — bookmark libraries are large enough that lossy imports can silently destroy their only useful organization.
photo-cull-review — storage cost and unusable photo noise demand reversible, human-reviewed cleanup rather than bulk deletion.
restore-drill — cloud copies are common, but people still cannot demonstrate that their backup can actually be restored.
confidential-file-handoff — sensitive-file senders need a recipient-friendly process and accurate security language, not another opaque sharing site.
rss-saved-queue — saved articles and RSS readers remain separate workflows despite active requests for a private bridge.
receipt-statement-linker — context captured at online checkout is lost by the time transactions reach a statement or accounting import.
accessible-table-ocr-check — OCR text alone does not make scanned tables usable for screen readers or reliable downstream reading.
tab-sunset-list — native groups store tab overload but do not prompt deliberate decisions about stale tabs across devices.
timezone-slot-proof — DST defects can offer meetings outside stated hours, while simple schedulers lack a transparent test view.
photo-exit-manifest — families leaving a photo cloud need a verifiable cutover plan, not just a bulk file export.
payout-reconciliation-explainer — small sellers can see mismatches but lack a transparent way to explain them to a bookkeeper.
csv-import-contract — migration teams need durable parsing and mapping agreements instead of undocumented spreadsheet cleanup.
