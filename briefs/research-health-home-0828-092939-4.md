# Health & home research — 2026-08-28

- care-visit-brief — illness trackers are repeatedly abandoned before users need an accurate appointment history.
- dose-count-compass — non-pill medicines expose a concrete remaining-dose and refill-risk gap.
- nutrient-floor-planner — people want meal planning around fibre/sugar/fat targets without a full calorie diary.
- batch-cart — multi-recipe serving changes still turn into error-prone hand-built shopping lists.
- recipe-passport — personal recipes need portable, calm offline reading as users leave ad-heavy pages and locked-in apps.
- garden-care-map — care reminders become more useful when tied to a real bed and irrigation layout.
- shop-fit-sheet — home makers need a small fit/clearance check before expensive one-off material purchases.
- home-service-passbook — recurring maintenance needs vendor-neutral history and correct recurrence rules, not more appliance apps.
- chore-receipt — shared chores work better when anyone can complete them and the outcome is recorded locally.
- placeboard-inventory — real household items live in multiple places, which single-location inventories model badly.
- bills-runway — people need a private 30–60-day cash answer without bank aggregation or full budgeting software.
- spend-pulse — finance-app churn and reminder requests favor a respectful, tiny offline spending-pace loop.
