# Games & creative research — 2026-08-27

- audio-wrapper-batch — repeatable branded audio batches are still assembled by hand, while creators also need portable setup.
- timing-calibrator — rhythm experiences need a clear answer to whether bad timing is in the track or the phone.
- natural-pause-recorder — threshold recording damages intentional pauses, and local browser speech handling is newly practical.
- audio-chain-cards — creators need shareable, reviewable repair procedures rather than opaque effect-preset folklore.
- cycle-block-animator — animators need dynamic loop blocks and games need those loops exported within web texture budgets.
- guided-inking-overlay — artists are explicitly asking for rotated vanishing and repeated spline-guide behavior outside heavyweight art apps.
- sprite-frame-lab — sprite regions are not exposed consistently across shaders and UI, inviting a visual UV test bench.
- remote-scene-cues — small interactive productions get tangled in camera and lobby state before they can rehearse.
- edit-trail-finder — non-destructive photo edits are valuable search data but remain hard to query across an archive.
- folder-recipe-switcher — mixed source folders need portable, inspectable processing intent rather than remembered editor state.
- tab-markup-playground — lightweight tab/chord writing and theory visualization are active, complementary requests.
- sing-to-controller — vocal gestures are an underused, privacy-preserving creative controller for games and learning tools.
