# Education opportunity research — 2026-08-28

context-cloze-vocab — learners are explicitly asking for contextual, productive vocabulary recall rather than isolated-card review.
return-ramp — interrupted study habits and punitive streaks are causing learners to abandon otherwise useful tools.
reading-margin-recall — readers need a low-friction, private bridge from selected passages to later recall.
podcast-recall-loop — educational podcast listening remains high-volume and low-retention without a tiny retrieval loop.
self-study-proofbook — deep self-study lacks a portable evidence trail that is more meaningful than course completion.
prerequisite-pathboard — self-learners need to repair missing conceptual dependencies without following an irrelevant full curriculum.
explanation-lab — tutors and learners need repeatable explanation and reflection practice, not more generated answers.
flex-practice-queue — learners want voluntary rehearsal in short windows without corrupting their SRS schedules.
lesson-code-room — remote coding teachers still need a no-account, low-failure first exercise room.
kid-code-missions — parents and tutors want an interest-led, age-appropriate bridge to programming logic.
trace-before-run — beginning programmers need prediction and tracing habits before they run or copy code.
in-class-draft-ticket — teachers need an authentic drafting process record without AI surveillance or lockdown software.
