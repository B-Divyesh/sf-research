# Small-business research — 2026-08-28

quote-revision-vault — Quote tools still lack a compact, customer-readable history when a freelancer changes scope or price.
quote-approval-receipt — Small teams need proof of the actual client approver when decisions happen outside a portal.
deposit-deadline-bridge — Deposit and final-payment dates are being lost when accepted quotes become invoices.
client-catalogue-request — Repeat B2B buyers need a structured quote-request path without a full ecommerce build.
mtd-evidence-pack — UK MTD pressure is forcing local-tool users into suites; an accountant-ready evidence handoff is a smaller escape valve.
unbilled-work-sweep — Completed work remains invisible until a weekly reconciliation catches it, delaying cash.
barcode-intake-card — Small inventory holders need privacy-preserving scan-to-item intake before they need an ERP.
stock-return-trail — Returning unused field stock is still an item-by-item history lookup rather than a job-closeout action.
paid-before-ship-gate — Tiny sellers want a deliberate payment hold before packing, not a broad order-management migration.
bills-due-board — Local finance users need a legible bill queue and manual paid transition without ledger workarounds.
subscription-renewal-calendar — Recurring-cost visibility fails on real schedules; owners need review reminders before renewals.
client-exit-packet — Current subcontractor workflows still lose the billed-versus-end-client context needed for a safe handoff.
