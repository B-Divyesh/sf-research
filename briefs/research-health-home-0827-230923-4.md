# Health / home opportunity research

rep-range-compass — Common double-progression tracking fails at the exact in-workout decision point.

meal-list-boundaries — Multi-home families need plans to yield correctly scoped shopping handoffs.

scaled-cook-card — Cooks still need scaled quantities embedded in the step they are following.

finish-one-pantry — Detailed inventory fails because it is too hard to keep synchronized; finishing a package is sustainable.

bed-season-ledger — Climate-aware garden planning needs an equally simple record of what actually occupied each bed.

home-care-evidence — Home maintenance needs durable why/done-proof/next-due records, not another generic task list.

claim-ready-homebook — Insurance evidence is valuable precisely when users do not want a permanent claim-service subscription.

chore-rulebook — Households need transparent allocation rules and effort history, not points or opaque assignments.

lan-family-dayboard — No-account shared family calendars point to a clear need for ambient local display.

bill-runway — People still use painful spreadsheets to answer whether upcoming obligations are covered before payday.

manual-spend-replay — Deliberate manual entry works best with fast repeats and a review view, not full bank-connected budgeting.

dose-chain-log — Multi-medication users need group confirmation and actual-time follow-up chains while keeping health data local.
