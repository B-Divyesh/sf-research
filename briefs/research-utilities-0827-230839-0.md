# Utilities research — 2026-08-27

photo-backup-sentinel — mobile photo copies fail silently; a local completeness and restore check has a narrow, high-trust job.
photo-exit-bundle — Google Photos users need a usable, metadata-aware archive when storage pressure and lockout anxiety force an exit.
archive-restore-rehearsal — large personal archives need proof of restoration, not one more way to make an untested copy.
pdf-flow-reader — low-vision knowledge workers still encounter PDF text flows that screen readers and zoom do not make workable.
scan-to-study-pack — locally converting scans into page-linked study text avoids repetitive cloud uploads and brittle OCR setups.
personal-data-exit-map — service exports remain opaque; a local inventory and preservation checklist makes departure actionable.
tab-context-capsule — overloaded and fragile browser sessions need a portable explanation of context, not just preserved URLs.
family-digital-dossier — families need a reviewed locator map for critical digital records without sharing every secret in advance.
feed-later-bridge — RSS users keep asking to move marked reading between tools without another proprietary queue.
reading-archive-pack — Pocket-style shutdowns create demand for a durable Markdown migration package, not a replacement silo.
csv-import-cleanroom — template imports still force nontechnical operators into destructive spreadsheet edits or one-off scripts.
pdf-accessibility-preflight — authors with no specialist budget need an understandable local check before inaccessible PDFs reach readers.
