# Small-business opportunity research — 2026-08-28

collections-ladder — overdue collection is a high-severity human workflow that generic reminders and manual WhatsApp do not manage safely.

reminder-mailroom — invoice reminders commonly pollute accounting archives; mail-type routing fixes a concrete reconciliation nuisance.

quote-review-gate — tiny agencies need a lightweight approval step before an incorrect price or duplicate quote reaches a client.

client-decision-receipt — replace passive proposal-view tracking with a clear, shared yes/no/change decision and scope snapshot.

subclient-worklog — subcontractors need billable proof that distinguishes the payer from the end client receiving the work.

client-request-catalog — repeat clients need a quote-first request surface without the cost and assumptions of a full online store.

tax-evidence-pack — the missing tax-prep artifact is a portable receipt/invoice evidence bundle, not another accounting ledger.

owner-cash-check — personal budgeting breaks down for business commitments, while large forecasting suites remain excessive for an owner’s weekly decision.

guest-booking-confirm — account-free booking and reliable confirmation state remain awkward across appointment stacks.

scan-count-pad — mobile/barcode stock counts remain stranded between spreadsheets and overly broad POS/ERP products.

stock-photo-aliases — helpers need visual recognition for package substitutions that map to one canonical stock item.

stocktake-reconcile — a simple unit-safe variance journal can make periodic physical counts auditable without replacing inventory software.
