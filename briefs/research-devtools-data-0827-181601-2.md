- schema-drift-snapshot — Database/ORM boundaries still produce difficult-to-explain production schema differences.
- supabase-exit-map — Builders are actively hitting managed-backend price cliffs and asking for controllable alternatives.

- flaky-test-casefile — Playwright flakiness remains a direct request, with raw retries and artifacts still hard to diagnose.
